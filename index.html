Concrete example

Imagine you run a small electrical contracting business.

Three large customers all want public liability insurance, workers compensation, a WHS policy and your electrical contractor licence — but each asks in a different format. SupplierProof is intended to become your reusable compliance workspace, independent of whichever portal each customer happens to use.

The whole point

The next customer starts with what you already know.

Your sample business profile and evidence library stay in this browser. Start another request and reuse them again. SupplierProof is testing whether that simple ownership shift can save suppliers from repeatedly rebuilding the same compliance pack.
